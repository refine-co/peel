Installing a pack

Three steps, about thirty seconds. You need Peel installed first.

  1. Download the pack. Tap Download .peel on the packs page. Your browser saves it to your Downloads folder. Chrome may warn that the file type is unusual — it is a zip of pictures, and Peel is the only app that reads it.
  2. Import it in Peel. Tap Open in Peel next to the download: Peel opens its file picker in your Downloads folder, with the pack you just saved right there. By hand works too — open Peel, tap New pack, scroll to the bottom of the sheet and tap Import a pack file. Peel asks you to confirm — “Peel Halloween, 20 stickers, will be added to your packs” — and checks every sticker against WhatsApp's rules before it adds anything.
  3. Add it to WhatsApp. Open the pack in Peel and tap Add to WhatsApp. It appears in your sticker tray straight away.

Putting one sticker on a TikTok, a Reel or a CapCut edit

A sticker pack belongs to WhatsApp. Video apps work differently: TikTok, CapCut, Instagram Stories and Canva all place a sticker by reading your gallery, so the sticker has to be saved there first. That is one tap in Peel.

  1. Save the sticker to your gallery. Open the pack in Peel, tap the sticker you want (press and hold, for an animated one), then tap Save to Photos. It lands in a Peel album: a still sticker as a transparent PNG, an animated one as a GIF.
  2. Add it in the video app. In TikTok: start your video, tap the sticker icon down the right-hand side, and the first tile in that panel opens your gallery — pick the sticker and tap OK. It drops onto the video as an overlay you can drag and resize, with the cut-out edge intact, and an animated one keeps moving. CapCut, Instagram Stories and Canva are the same idea: add an image or overlay, then pick from the Peel album.
  3. Skip “Upload to remove background” if TikTok offers it. Peel has already cut the subject out on your phone, and that option would send your picture to TikTok's servers to do it again.

Save to Photos needs the next Peel update. It is built and waiting for the next release, and it needs Android 10 or newer. The packs on this site work with the version you have today — this one section is the part that arrives with the update.

If tapping the download does not open Peel

It usually will not, and that is the browser's doing rather than Peel's. Chrome records the file as plain bytes and hands Peel a link with no filename in it, so Peel cannot tell it is a pack. Two ways around it, both one tap:

  • Open in Peel, beside each download on the packs page. Peel opens its picker in Downloads.
  • Share it. In your Downloads list, or in the chat someone sent it to you in, choose Share and pick Peel.

Either way Peel reads the file, checks it against WhatsApp's rules, and asks before adding anything.

Why a file and not a button in the app

Peel has no internet permission at all. It is not switched off, it is absent — you can see that for yourself in the app's permission list and in its Play Data safety entry. That is the whole point of Peel: your photos cannot be uploaded anywhere, because the app has no way to reach the network. The cost of that promise is this one: packs arrive as a file your browser downloads, rather than from inside the app.
