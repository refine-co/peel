Your photos,
cut into stickers.

Peel turns a photo into a sticker pack for WhatsApp or Telegram in under a minute, and saves any sticker to your gallery for a TikTok, a Reel or a CapCut edit. The cutting out happens on your phone — your photos are never uploaded, and nothing you make leaves it unless you choose to send it in.

Why people keep it

1

Your photos stay put

The cut-out runs on your phone and nothing you make is uploaded. Peel reaches the network only when you ask it to — downloading a pack or scene you tapped, or fetching the list when you open More fun or pull it down — from this site and nowhere else. The privacy policy spells it out.

2

It is fast

Pick a photo, Peel finds the subject and cuts it out. Thirty text stickers come out of a single tap.

3

Moving stickers too

A short video becomes an animated sticker, inside WhatsApp's size limit, without you thinking about it.

4

It goes where you post

A whole pack into WhatsApp or Telegram. A single transparent sticker saved to your gallery for a TikTok, an Instagram Reel or a CapCut edit. How that works.
