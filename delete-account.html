Delete your Peel account

Peel only has an account if you chose to send a pack in. If you never did that, there is nothing here to delete — making stickers, downloading packs and everything else in Peel works without an account.

Last updated 18 October 2026

In the app, in three taps

This is the quickest way and it happens immediately, with no waiting and no message to anyone.

  1. Open any pack you made.
  2. Tap the three dots, then Send this to Peel.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

By email, if you cannot open the app

Write to refine.co.my@gmail.com from the Google address you signed in with, and say you want your Peel account deleted. We use that address only to find your account and to confirm when it is gone. Expect a reply within seven days.

What is deleted

  • Your name and email address.
  • The Google account identifier that signed you in.
  • Every sign-in token, so all your devices are signed out.
  • Any pack still waiting to be reviewed — both the file and its record.

This is immediate and cannot be undone. Nothing is kept in a backup copy for later.

What stays, and why

A pack of yours that was already published stays on the site and in the app. Other people have downloaded it, and withdrawing a pack from their phones is not something we can do. It is no longer connected to you: the record is pointed at an anonymous placeholder, so nothing links it to your name, your address or your Google account. The credit line printed on the pack is the one you typed when you sent it in; if you want that changed or the pack taken down entirely, say so in the same email and we will do it.

The app tells you how many published packs this applies to before you confirm, so there is no surprise.

Removing a pack without deleting your account

You do not have to delete your account to take something back.

  • A pack still waiting to be reviewed: write to refine.co.my@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, naming the pack. The file and its record are deleted and nothing of it is kept.
  • A pack already published: same address, same thing. We take it off the site and out of the catalogue. Copies already downloaded to other people's phones stay there — nobody can reach into those — but it stops being offered.
  • Just the credit line: say what it should read instead, or ask for it to be removed, and we change it.

Your account, your name and your address are untouched by any of this. Expect a reply within seven days.

Deleting the app instead

Uninstalling Peel removes everything the app holds on your phone — your packs, your stickers and your sign-in. It does not delete the account on our side. Use one of the two routes above for that.
