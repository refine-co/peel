If you have made a pack in Peel that other people would enjoy, email it to us. It goes up on the packs page for anyone to download, with your name on it.

Email your pack What can go up

Sending one

1

Export it

In Peel, long-press your pack and choose Share pack file. You get a .peel file — a zip holding every sticker, its emoji tags and the tray icon.

2

Email it

To refine.co.my@gmail.com, with the name you want credited and one line about the pack.

3

Wait for a reply

A person looks at every pack before it goes up, so this is days rather than minutes. If it is not a fit, you get a reply saying why.

What can go up

Yes

  • Your own drawings, photos and lettering
  • Your own pets
  • Your own characters
  • Between 3 and 30 stickers, which is what WhatsApp allows

No

  • Brands, logos and company characters
  • Film, TV, game and anime characters
  • Celebrities and public figures
  • Photographs of people who have not agreed
  • Anything sexual, hateful or aimed at a particular person
  • Children
Most of the “no” list is one rule in different clothes: if someone else owns it, or someone else is in it without saying yes, it cannot go up.

What you are agreeing to

By sending a pack you confirm you made it, that you have the right to share it, and that we may publish it on this site for other people to download for free, with your credit on it. You keep ownership of your artwork. Ask us to take it down at any time and we will, by replying to your own email.

No uploads, no account

There is no upload form here on purpose. Peel collects nothing and this site runs no server, so a pack reaches us only if you deliberately email it. Nothing is published automatically.
