If you have made a pack in Peel that other people would enjoy, send it from inside the app. It goes up on the packs page for anyone to download, with your name on it.

How to send one What can go up

Sending one

1

Put your name on it, then export it

Open the pack in Peel, tap the three dots, and choose Name and credit. Whatever you put under Credited to is the name that gets published with the pack — and the one WhatsApp shows under the pack title. It starts as “Peel”, so change it to yours.

Then, from the same menu, choose Share pack file. You get a .peel file — a zip holding every sticker, its emoji tags and the tray icon.

2

Send it from the app

From the same three-dot menu, choose Send this to Peel. The first time, Peel asks for a name and an email address and sends you a six-digit code — that is the whole account, and it exists so a pack can be credited to somebody and so a vote counts once. You can set a password afterwards, or keep using a code each time.

Then type the credit name, one line about the pack, tick the box saying the work is yours to give, and send. Nothing leaves your phone until you press that button.

Rather not make an account? Choose Share pack file instead and email the .peel to refine.co.my@gmail.com with the same two things. It goes through exactly the same reading.

3

Wait for a reply

One person looks at every pack before it goes up, around a day job, so this takes as long as it takes — days, sometimes longer. If it is not a fit, you get a reply saying why.

What can go up

Yes

  • Your own drawings, photos and lettering
  • Your own pets
  • Your own characters
  • Between 3 and 30 stickers, which is what WhatsApp allows

No

  • Brands, logos and company characters
  • Film, TV, game and anime characters
  • Celebrities and public figures
  • Photographs of people who have not agreed
  • Anything sexual, hateful or aimed at a particular person
  • Children
Most of the “no” list is one rule in different clothes: if someone else owns it, or someone else is in it without saying yes, it cannot go up.

What you are agreeing to

By sending a pack you confirm you made it, that you have the right to share it, and that we may publish it on this site for other people to download for free, with your credit on it. You keep ownership of your artwork. Ask us to take it down at any time and we will — reply to the email Peel sent you, or write to refine.co.my@gmail.com from the address you signed up with.

What the account is, and is not

A name and an email address, nothing else — no password required, no profile, no sign-in anywhere else in Peel. It exists only so a published pack can carry somebody's name and so a vote counts once per person. Your address is never shown to anyone and is never used to write to you about anything but the pack you sent. You can delete the account, and everything still waiting with it, from the same screen; what the privacy policy says about that is what happens.

Nothing is published automatically. Every pack waits for a person to read it.
