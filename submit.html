Share your pack

If you have made something in Peel you are happy for other people to use, send it to us and it goes up on the packs page, credited to you.

How to send one

  1. Export it. In Peel, long-press your pack and choose Share pack file. You get a .peel file.
  2. Email it to refine.co.my@gmail.com, with the name you want credited and one line about the pack.
  3. Wait for a reply. Every pack is looked at by a person before it goes up, so it is days rather than minutes. If it is not a fit you get a reply saying why.

What gets published

YesYour own drawings, photos and lettering. Your own pets. Your own characters.
NoBrands, logos and company characters. Film, TV, game and anime characters. Celebrities and public figures. Photographs of people who have not agreed. Anything sexual, hateful or aimed at a particular person. Children.
SizeBetween 3 and 30 stickers, which is what WhatsApp allows.

Most of the "no" list is one rule in different clothes: if someone else owns it, or someone else is in it without saying yes, it cannot go up.

What you are agreeing to

By sending a pack you confirm you made it, that you have the right to share it, and that we may publish it on this site for other people to download for free, with your credit on it. You keep ownership of your artwork. Ask us to take it down at any time and we will, by replying to your own email.

No uploads, no account

There is no upload form here on purpose. Peel collects nothing and this site runs no server, so a pack reaches us only if you deliberately email it. Nothing is published automatically.
