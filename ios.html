Peel · iPhone

There is no iPhone version of Peel

Peel is an Android app, and today that is the whole of it. If somebody sent you a sticker pack and you could not open it, that is why.

Should I build one?

That is a real question and I do not know the answer yet. Peel is made by one person in Kuala Lumpur, and an iPhone version is months of work on top of the Android one, so I would rather find out whether anyone wants it before spending them.

So: leave your email. That is the whole vote.

What you are agreeing to

  • No money. This is not a pre-order and there is nothing to pay. If that ever changes it will be on the App Store, like anything else.
  • One message. You will hear once, when there is something to try. Not a newsletter.
  • Or you will hear that it is not happening. If too few people ask, I will write and say so rather than leave you waiting.
  • Your address and nothing else. No name, no phone number. It is used for that one message and is not sold, shared or added to anything.

No date, and I am not going to invent one

Nothing is written, nothing is in review and there is no Apple developer account yet. Anyone who gives you a date for an app that has not been started is guessing. When there is a build, the people on this list are the first to know.

Meanwhile

If you are on Android, Peel is free on Google Play. If you are not, the sticker packs on this site are plain WhatsApp packs and the how to install page covers getting them in by hand.
