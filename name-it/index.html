Put your name on a
Halloween sticker pack.

Type a name. Four animated stickers are made with it, right on your phone — nothing is uploaded, and there is no account.

Download the pack Open in Peel

  2. Tap Open in Peel, then choose the pack you just downloaded. No Peel yet? That button takes you to it on Google Play.
  3. Tap through to add it to WhatsApp.
