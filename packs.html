Scenes

A scene is a picture with its own stickers lifted out of it. You get the empty place and the things that belong in it, and you put them back — the far ones first, so the near ones land on top. Tap a piece below to put it down, or press Fill it in and watch the picture come back.

Village Night

37 pieces · a valley after dark, seven cottages and a stream

The furthest things first — the clouds, the mountains, the forest — then the village, then the stones, then the flowers and the butterflies. The picture comes back in the order it was painted.

Every window is lit. The valley can sleep.

Lantern Alley

20 pieces · a wet alley, paper lanterns and the light on the ground

Five chapters, far to near: the end of the alley, then the walls, then what hangs over the street, then what stands on it, and the sleeping cat last. Rain falls while you work, and the puddles catch whatever you have lit.

The lamps are lit. The alley can rest.
