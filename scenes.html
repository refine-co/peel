Scenes

A scene is a picture with its own stickers lifted out of it. You get the empty place and the things that belong in it, and you put them back — the far ones first, so the near ones land on top. Tap a piece below to put it down, or press Fill it in and watch the picture come back.

6 scenes. Swipe to see them all.

Knock Twice Needs the unlock

40 pieces · a dark cottage, and everything you hang on it

The only scene that is not about putting something back. The house is painted undressed and stays that way for eleven months of the year; Halloween is what you build on it. Forty pieces, from the pumpkins on the path to the witch crossing the moon.

The house is dressed. Let them come knocking.
